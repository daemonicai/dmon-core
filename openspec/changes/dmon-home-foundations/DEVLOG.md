- **Resolved:**
  - **Q5 — device-key provisioning ownership** (Product Owner, 2026-08-02): `dmon-home` **self-provisions**; the `ndmon` provisioning-verb alternative is not taken, so no prerequisite change lands ahead of section 6. Task 6.5 stands as written, and its pinned-digest test is what contains the two-language hash coupling. If the host is ever split into a `LaunchDaemon` under a different principal, or the gateway takes a non-loopback bind (which ADR-036 fail-closes), this lapses and provisioning must be revisited.

Decision: **record, do not foreclose, do not build.** Sections 1–10 stay co-located. Captured in design.md → "Known future topology" with a per-concern table.
